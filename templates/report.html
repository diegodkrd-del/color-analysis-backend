The Science & History

Seasonal color analysis originated in the early 20th century, pioneered by Bauhaus master Johannes Itten. Itten observed that his art students naturally gravitated toward pigments matching their personal hair, eye, and skin tones. Today, our engine uses precise CIE L*a*b* color space transformations and Individual Typology Angle (ITA°) calculations to scientifically match your natural light reflection.
